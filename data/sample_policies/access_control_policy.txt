Access Control Policy (Sample)

Purpose: This policy establishes requirements for managing user access to
information systems that process sensitive customer data.

Requirements:
1. All privileged and administrative accounts must be provisioned only
   after documented manager approval.
2. Multi-factor authentication is required for all privileged accounts
   and for any remote access into the production environment.
3. Access to systems containing sensitive data must be reviewed on a
   quarterly basis by the control owner, with results documented and
   retained for audit purposes.
4. User accounts must be disabled within 24 hours of termination as
   communicated by Human Resources.
5. Access requests are logged and subject to periodic internal audit.
