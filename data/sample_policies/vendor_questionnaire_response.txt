Vendor Security Questionnaire Response (Sample)

Q: Describe your encryption practices for data at rest and in transit.
A: All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit
using TLS 1.2 or higher. Key management is handled via a dedicated
hardware security module with quarterly key rotation.

Q: Describe your incident response process.
A: We maintain a documented incident response plan tested annually via
tabletop exercise. All security incidents are logged, triaged within
one hour, and escalated per severity.

Q: How do you manage third-party/subcontractor risk?
A: All subcontractors with access to customer data undergo a security
due diligence review prior to onboarding and annually thereafter.

Q: Describe your vulnerability management program.
A: We perform monthly vulnerability scans and quarterly penetration
tests, with critical findings remediated within 15 days.
